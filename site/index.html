Geometry HydroRIVERS v1.0 (HydroSHEDS, WWF), reaches with a catchment of 5 km² or more. Flow direction comes from the network itself. Discharge BAFU gauging stations, read live through the federal Linked Data service LINDAS, updated every ten minutes. A reach with no gauge takes the long-term mean discharge of the reach, scaled by how far the nearest gauge downstream stands from its own mean, or failing that the largest gauged river above it; those reaches are drawn dimmer. A reach that drains out of the country past no gauge at all is drawn in grey and carries its long-term mean only. Nine stations report in litres per second; those readings are converted. Measured and modelled water, not a forecast.
