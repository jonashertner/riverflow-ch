Three places where this data meets Swiss law

The provisions are quoted from the consolidated German texts on fedlex.admin.ch, in the versions in force on 19 August 2026. A datum below is a fact about a river or a glacier. It is not a finding of breach. The step from one to the other is the lawyer's, and it needs the concession, the licence and the site.

1. Residual flow. Water Protection Act, Art. 31(1) SR 814.20

Where water is taken from a watercourse with a permanent flow, a minimum must remain in the bed. The figure follows from Q347, defined in Art. 4(h) as the discharge that, averaged over ten years, is reached or exceeded on 347 days of the year, and that is not substantially affected by damming, abstraction or diversion.

Computed from the table in Art. 31(1). Each band starts from the figure the statute states for it and rises at the rate the statute gives. The bands do not close exactly: a rate carried up to a boundary and the figure the statute states at that boundary differ by a fraction of a litre, 279.6 against 280 at 500 l/s and 2497.5 against 2500 at 10 000 l/s. That is the statute's own arithmetic, reproduced and not smoothed. The table begins at 50 l/s and stops at 10 000 l/s from a Q347 of 60 000 l/s. The result is a floor, not a permission: Art. 31(2) requires the figure to be raised where water quality, groundwater recharge, rare habitats, fish passage or spawning grounds are not otherwise secured, and Arts. 32 and 33 govern the exceptions and the weighing of interests. Q347 is not published per gauge in the live federal feed, so it is entered here, not read from the map.

2. Temperature. Waters Protection Ordinance, Annex 2 No. 12(4) SR 814.201

Die Temperatur eines Fliessgewässers darf durch Wärmeeintrag oder ‑entzug gegenüber dem möglichst unbeeinflussten Zustand um höchstens 3 °C, in Gewässerabschnitten der Forellenregion um höchstens 1,5 °C, verändert werden; dabei darf die Wassertemperatur 25 °C nicht übersteigen. Diese Anforderungen gelten nach weitgehender Durchmischung.

The rule governs alteration by heat input or removal, measured against the least affected state, and it applies after substantial mixing. A river that is warm without a heat source is not in breach of it. The temperature layer therefore shows where the question arises. It does not answer it. To answer it you need the unaffected reference state and the discharge point.

3. Ecological goals. Waters Protection Ordinance, Annex 1 No. 1(3)(a) SR 814.201

Die Wasserqualität soll so beschaffen sein, dass: a. die Temperaturverhältnisse naturnah sind.

A goal, not a limit value. It carries weight in the weighing of interests and in the reading of the numerical requirements, and it is the provision the glacier record speaks to most directly: a catchment that has lost half its ice does not have a near-natural thermal or flow regime, whatever any single gauge reads today.

What the glacier record is good for

The 1850 and 2023 inventories are the same office measuring the same objects by the methods of their day. They are strong evidence of a change of state in a catchment and of its size and its timing. They are not, on their own, evidence that any named party caused it. Causation and attribution are separate questions and need separate sources.
