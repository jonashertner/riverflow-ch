A filled mark carries a quantity; an open ring locates an object only. Hydropower design discharge is derived from capacity and head, not measured.     The water cycle, read as evidence

    Follow the water. Protect its pathways.

    Trace water through Switzerland—from ice and groundwater to rivers, storage, use, return and the border—and identify the law protecting each essential pathway. The aim is to protect wetlands and enable legal action when those protections fail. This first release focuses on surface water. Every figure states its source, date and evidence class.
