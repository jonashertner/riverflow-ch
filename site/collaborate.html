Public collaboration

Help the water tell its whole story.

A stream, a wetland and a laboratory result are parts of one water cycle. Riverflow turns local knowledge, official records and specialist review into a shared evidence base—so people can find what is known, what is missing and what can be protected next.

Public research questions last checked 23.08.2026. A listed question is not a finding of breach.

How you can help now

You do not need to complete an investigation. The most useful contribution is often one precise observation or one primary record, tied to a place and date. Choose the route that matches what you actually know.

Local knowledge

Record an observation

Report a drain, dry area, barrier, discharge, restoration measure or change you observed. State when and where; separate what you saw from what you think it means.

Submit an observation

Public administration

Add an official record

Point to a protection instrument, plan, permit, monitoring series, consultation or restoration report. Give the issuing authority, date and stable public link.

Science and data

Review the evidence

Check a measurement, method, join, unit, model or missing dataset. Include a reproducible check and say what the evidence can—and cannot—show.

Two connected campaigns

Active · water cycle

Keep water in the landscape

Wetlands depend on more than an inventory boundary. Their hydrology can also depend on surrounding drains, groundwater, inflows, land use and restoration. The current work asks where the operative protection instrument, functional buffers and field evidence can be brought together without treating a model as a legal boundary.

  • Connect each national wetland object to its current protection instrument.
  • Keep hydrological, nutrient and disturbance functions separate.
  • Record restoration and current planning as evidence, including evidence of progress.

Open · water quality

Follow a substance to its context

Riverflow already publishes station-level NAWA results without interpolation or a composite score. The next collaborative step is to add the denser cantonal networks and connect a result to sampling method, flow conditions, possible inputs, permits and authority assessments—without turning concentration alone into a compliance claim.

  • Locate downloadable cantonal station series and their reuse terms.
  • Preserve exact laboratory rows, censoring and determination limits.
  • Separate a measured result from source attribution and legal assessment.

Places and questions—not verdicts

These starting points come from current official records. They show where a bounded public contribution could close an evidence gap. They do not rank cantons, establish unlawfulness or replace coordination with organisations and authorities already involved.

Zürich · object question

Official record

Neeracherried

What the public record says. The Zürich government reported in 2025 that the 1956 ordinance covers only the core fen, that ecologically adequate buffers are outstanding and that a revision is being coordinated with a road project. Early public participation for the road project is announced for 2027.

What would help. Public drafts and maps of the revised instrument; separate hydrological, nutrient and disturbance assessments; drainage and water-level evidence; restoration and interim measures; and the original notice when a participation period opens.

Primary records: RRB 1302/2025 · road project

Schwyz · programme question

Official record

Protection instruments and buffers

What the public record says. The canton’s 2026 nature-protection concept describes deficits at contract-only national wetland objects and schedules generally binding rules and buffer work over several years. This is a cantonal programme statement, not an object-specific finding.

What would help. For one object at a time: the operative plan or contract, exact inventory identity, current buffer and hydrology material, third-party effects, measures already taken and any live procedure. Recreation-pressure objects named in the concept are useful records targets.

Primary record: Nature-protection concept, May 2026

Bern · method comparator

Model workflow

Moor hydrological perimeter

What the public record says. Bern publishes the federal moor-hydrological indication perimeter as a screening product. If effects cannot be excluded, the workflow calls for specialist review or a hydrological report. The perimeter is not presented as a binding buffer.

What would help. Test the same transparent triage elsewhere: model indication first, object and instrument check second, field validation third. Document where the workflow resolves a concern as carefully as where it identifies a remaining question.

Primary records: geoproduct · work aid

Why these three? One concrete object, one cantonal programme and one positive method comparator make the first public work testable without converting a nationwide implementation concern into a nationwide accusation.

From contribution to protection

  1. 1

    Locate

    Name the place, waterbody, station or protected object and the date relevant to your contribution.

  2. 2

    Classify

    Say whether it is a measurement, field observation, official register, model, legal text or inference.

  3. 3

    Trace

    Add the original source, identifier, version, licence and smallest proposition it supports.

  4. 4

    Review

    A maintainer checks reproducibility and scope. Scientific and legal review remain separate and independent.

  5. 5

    Publish or hold

    Accepted evidence enters the public record with its limit. Sensitive, personal or unresolved material stays out.

  6. 6

    Protect

    Only an object-specific record can support dialogue, participation, restoration or legal assessment. Riverflow itself does not declare a breach.

How contributions are labelled

The label follows the evidence, not the importance of the concern. Status can change after review, but the original contribution and the reason for the change remain visible in the repository.

LabelWhat it establishesWhat it does not establish
MeasurementA value reported for a method, place and time.Cause, representativeness or legal compliance.
Field observationWhat a contributor saw, where and when.Hydrological effect or responsibility without validation.
Official recordWhat an authority published or decided.That every statement in the record is independently verified.
Model or screenA reproducible indication under stated assumptions.A measured condition, binding boundary or breach.
InferenceA reasoned question or connection between evidence.The underlying facts beyond its cited support.
Not assessedThat relevant evidence has not yet been reviewed here.That the evidence does not exist or a duty was not met.

Publish with care

GitHub issues are public. Do not publish personal data, private correspondence, access credentials, the exact location of sensitive species or material whose disclosure could cause harm. Remove image metadata when it is not needed. Obtain consent before identifying another person.

For security issues or evidence that should not be public, use the repository’s private security reporting channel and describe the safe contact route before sending sensitive material.
